About

Johannesburg

STATIC is run by Ruan Bester, a self-taught developer in Johannesburg. Everything here is drawn, wrapped and tested by one person — the designs, the 3D previewer on this site, and the site itself.

The brand rule is simple: a classic template should survive being looked at from behind. Most cheap Roblox clothing puts a graphic on the chest and leaves the back, the shoulders and the undersides of the sleeves blank. These don't. Prints wrap, stripes meet at the seams, and the all-over pieces are continuous across every face of the torso and both limbs.

Every template is 585 × 559, the classic clothing size, and every PNG on this site is the real file — download it, drop it into Roblox Studio, or wear it on the mannequin here first.
